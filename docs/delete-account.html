Delete your PetPals Connect account

This page is for PetPals Connect (package com.petpalsconnect.app), published by PetPalsConnectApp LLC. You can delete your account and the data that goes with it from inside the app, or by asking us if you no longer have the app.

In the app

  1. Open the More tab and choose Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more so we can revoke that sign-in.

Deletion happens immediately. You are signed out and the account cannot be recovered.

Without the app

Email contact@petpalsconnectapp.com with the subject line "Delete my PetPals Connect account" from the email address on the account (or the phone number, if you signed in by phone). We will confirm it is you, delete the account, and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: username, email address or phone number, profile photo, settings and preferences.
  • Your pets: their profiles, photos and health records.
  • Your conversations and the messages and photos in them, your group chat memberships and your messages in groups.
  • Your playdates, invitations, matches, swipes, favourites, pals and pal requests, reviews and notifications.
  • Your sign-in with Firebase Authentication, and your Sign in with Apple grant if you used it.
  • Your device's push notification token.

What is kept, and why

  • Safety reports you made or that were made about you, for up to three years, so that patterns of abuse can still be seen across accounts. They reference your former account by an internal id only.
  • Support messages you sent us, for up to three years, as a record of what was said in case of a dispute.
  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own policies. We do not hold your payment details.
  • Copies in our service providers' backups are removed on their schedules (Google Firebase: within 180 days).

Details are in our Privacy Policy. If you have an active subscription, cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings too - deleting the account does not stop the store from billing you.

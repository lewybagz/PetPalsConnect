In short.

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your sign-in details, your pets' profiles and photos, your location (only with your permission), your messages and playdates, and a push-notification token for your device.

5. Location, in detail

Precise geolocation is sensitive under most US state privacy laws and under Canadian law, so here is exactly what happens. The app asks your phone for permission the first time you open a feature that needs your position (the map or scheduling a playdate) - never on launch. If you allow it, the app reads your current position and sends it to our servers once per session; we keep only the latest one. Distances shown to you and to others are calculated from it. On the map, other users see your dog's pin at a position rounded to about one kilometre; your exact coordinates are never sent to another user. You can turn off "Show me on the map" in Settings > Privacy, revoke the permission in your phone's settings at any time, and delete your stored position by deleting your account or emailing us. Emergency numbers in the care hub are shown without any location.

To exercise a right, email contact@petpalsconnectapp.com from the address on your account, or use the in-app controls (Settings covers your data directly).

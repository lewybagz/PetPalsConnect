You are meeting a stranger in a park.

That is the honest description of what this app arranges, and it is what the safety design starts from. Here is exactly what other people can see.

What another owner sees

Your dog leads. You are the line underneath, because at some point you do have to know who you are meeting.

Your dog

Name, photos, breed, size, age, temperament, and whether you have entered any vaccination dates.

Roughly where

A distance, and on the map a position rounded to about a kilometre. Never the exact place your phone last was.

Your username

Shown under your dog's name, as "with @you". Not your email, not your phone number, not your real name unless you make it your username.

Location, specifically

Sharing your position is optional and the app explains what it is for before the phone asks. If you say no, you stay in the deck with no distance shown rather than disappearing.

If you say yes, what other people receive is deliberately blunted. The stored position is wherever you last opened the app, which for most people is their house, so it is rounded to roughly a kilometre before it ever leaves our server. A map that hands strangers an exact address is not a feature.

You can turn it off afterwards, in settings, and the map stops showing you.

Blocking and reporting

Both live on the card and in the chat header, which are the two places you meet somebody new. Not buried three levels into a settings menu you would have to go looking for.

Blocking is total, and mutual

Block somebody and you vanish from each other everywhere at once: the deck, the map, search, and any chat between you. If they had several dogs, all of them go. You can see and undo your blocks in settings.

Reporting blocks at the same time

Filing a report blocks the person in the same action. Asking somebody who has just said they feel unsafe to then go and separately block the person is not something to ship.

Refusals never say who

When something is unavailable because of a block, the app says it is not available. It does not say "they blocked you", because telling a harasser exactly that is telling them what to do next.

Two things we are straight about

Vaccination status is what an owner typed

The badge on a card says "owner-reported" and it means it. Nobody at PetPals has checked a certificate. It is useful, it is not proof, and the app never calls it verified.

The collar is not a safety device

The tracking collar shows where it last reported from, and how long ago. That is the claim, in the app and here and in the terms. It does not find a lost pet and we will not describe it as though it does.

If something goes wrong

Report it in the app and a person looks at it. If several different people report the same account, it is hidden automatically while that happens, because that has to work at three in the morning when nobody is watching.

You can also write to us directly at contact@petpalsconnectapp.com, and you can delete your account and everything attached to it from inside the app at any time.

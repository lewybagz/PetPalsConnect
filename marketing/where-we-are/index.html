The whole state, and nowhere else.

PetPals is open across Arizona. If you are somewhere else, you can still sign up, still use the care hub, and still tell us where to go next.

Why one state

The apps like this that came before launched nationally, spread a few thousand people across a continent, and handed everybody an empty screen. Two dogs forty miles apart are not going to meet.

So this one starts in one place and stays there until it is worth being elsewhere. One state is small enough to actually tell whether it works.

Which ZIP codes count

Arizona's postal codes run from 850 to 865, with four gaps the postal service never assigned. All of it is in.

850 - 853

Phoenix, Mesa, Chandler, Glendale, Scottsdale, Casa Grande and Yuma.

855 - 857

Tucson, Sierra Vista and the south.

859 - 860

Show Low, Flagstaff and the high country.

863 - 865

Prescott, Kingman, Lake Havasu and the northwest.

The app asks for your ZIP once, when you make your profile. It is how the app knows which side of the line you are on, and it is the only thing that tells us where people are waiting. Sharing your location is a separate choice and always optional.

If you are outside Arizona

You are not turned away. You can make an account, add your pets, and use the whole care hub: vets and groomers near you, reminders, weights, vaccination records, the poison lookup. None of that depends on anybody else being nearby.

What you will not have is a deck of dogs to meet, because there is nobody there yet. Rather than filling it with dogs three states away, it stays empty and says so.

Join the list with your ZIP and you become the reason we open there. That is genuinely how the decision gets made.
